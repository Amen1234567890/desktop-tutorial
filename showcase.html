Featured Business Project

Firestone Pizza Co. Business Strategy Project

The Firestone Pizza Co. project was created as a realistic business case study to demonstrate business analysis, strategic thinking, customer engagement, and operational planning. The project focused on examining the challenges faced by a modern pizza business operating within a highly competitive market. The project explored areas including business operations, customer satisfaction, service quality, marketing opportunities, growth strategies, and digital innovation. Through detailed analysis, several recommendations were developed to improve business performance, efficiency, and customer engagement. One of the key outcomes of the project was the development of interactive website features such as a booking system and customer feedback platform. These elements highlighted how digital technologies can improve customer experience while supporting operational efficiency and business growth.
